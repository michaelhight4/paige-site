## Starters (offered by Revival Tech in Brookhollow)
### Earth line — Tumbloam → Burrowmane → Bastonox (Earth / stone)
- **Tumbloam**: pill-bug/wombat pup, slate moss-speckled shell bands, stone nose horn, violet tail crystal. Stubborn, loyal, curls up when shy. Lore: rolls down Brookhollow riverbanks packing soil into burrows.
- **Burrowmane**: grows a moss mane; protective older-sibling energy. Sunmeadow/Thornwood.
- **Bastonox**: fortress-shelled tank, violet crystal clusters on shoulders. Patient guardian; legends say a herd once walled a valley. Greystone Caverns.
### Fire line — Cindrip → Emberlunx → Forgeharrow (Fire / magma)
- **Cindrip**: charcoal tapir calf with lava-coal plates, violet veins. Curious, sniffs everything, sneezes sparks. Cindervale.
- **Emberlunx**: lean deer-legged tapir, coal spine crest. Restless sprinter.
- **Forgeharrow**: elk-sized, ember-venting spine & shoulder crystals. Proud, honourable; smiths once used its breath to fire kilns.
### Water line — Rillop → Brookhorn → Tidemarrow (Water / fresh water → sea)
- **Rillop**: seafoam otter-cuttlefish pup, frill-fin skirt, tendril whiskers. Playful, mischievous. Brookhollow river.
- **Brookhorn**: grows coral horns; brave, show-off. Fernwick/Saltmere.
- **Tidemarrow**: branching coral horns strung with violet beads, wing fins. Calm, wise; Tidewater Coast sailors read its moods as weather.

## Characters
- **Player (boy) — default "Cal" (proposal)**: messy dark hair, olive hooded field coat, satchel with violet crystal, blank ID badge. Revival Tech employee promoted to field researcher.
- **Player (girl) — default "Mira" (proposal)**: auburn ponytail, shorter olive jacket, cargo shorts over leggings, same kit.
- **Ron (rival / roommate)**: lanky, spiky sandy hair, rust jacket + navy utility vest, belt crystal. Friendly, cocky, co-op-capable rival from Brookhollow.
- **Director Hollis Vane (mentor / boss — proposed name)**: silver bob/bun, rimless glasses, plum-charcoal coat with violet piping, crystal ring & brooch. Promotes the player; polished, ambiguous — hook for the opposition's unethical-testing claims. Revival Heights.
- **The Riverbank Stranger (third NPC — proposed)**: the trainer from the player's river dream; weathered wanderer with grey braid, moss/river cloak, driftwood staff with violet crystal. Releases creatures' light rather than dominating — possible link to opposition / Legendary Guardian.
