Coming soon: what Paige Games is working on next

Hey, Gary here. Besides the games you can play right now, I've always got a few more worlds in the works. These are still in development, so I can't promise dates, and details may change as they come together. But I'd rather share them early and build them with you watching. Here's a peek.

Life of a Trash Mob: Slime World

In development

You're a slime, the little monster every hero smacks on the way to the real fight. Your mom's bedtime story about a legendary slime who beat grand dragons won't leave your head. Rise from trash mob to legend through team monster battles, sects, dojos and tournaments, then come home to farm, craft, dig underground and build up your base. Read more about Slime World

Bellmont: Life or Death

In development

Your parents left a debt, and the loan shark wants it paid. Every 7 days rent and the loan come due, the townsfolk are shady, and every favour you do cuts both ways. Work odd jobs, earn trust, learn a trade, and maybe one day open a shop of your own in a 3D low-poly town with a forest that feels alive. Read more about Bellmont
