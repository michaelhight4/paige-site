Pokémon world routes ← Route 101 Route 103 → Route 102 (Japanese: １０２番道路 Route 102 ) is a route in western Hoenn , connecting Oldale Town and Petalburg City . Contents Collapse all Hide Close table of contents (Return to top) In the games Route description Items Pokémon Generation III Generation VI Trainers Pokémon Ruby and Sapphire Pokémon Emerald Pokémon Omega Ruby and Alpha Sapphire Layout Music In animation Pokémon the Series Pokémon the Series: Ruby and Sapphire Related articles In the games Route description From Oldale Town, the route heads southwest, passing a small pond, a patch of grass and a Trainer. After crossing paths with Youngster Calvin, the route turns northwest, straddles a pair of grass patches, in which the player can face Bug Catcher Rick, and passes Youngster Allen. North of this patch, the route comes to a T-intersection. Lass Tiana can be found on the west path and also leads the player to Petalburg City , while the east path leads to a soft soil patch containing Oran and Pecha Berries . South of the final grass patch is a small area of grass enclosed by large trees. Located amidst the grass is a Potion .
