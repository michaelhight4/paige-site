Boundwake

▶ Play Boundwake All games

Alpha, free to play in your browser at paigegames.net/boundwake/. Things will change as it grows.

Boundwake is a fantasy life sim. You age a year at a time, train skills, awaken mana, enter tournaments, choose a path, and walk the realms. Phone stays stacked parchment; PC fills a multi-column layout.

What you can do in alpha

  • Train skills with clear lock reasons. Soft skills from about age 2; martial from 6; sword styles and your path at 8.
  • Awaken mana in life (or at an Academy visit), then practice Boundwake magic schools and see a mana bar.
  • Tournaments every 3 human-years — blade, spark, and craft meets in brackets 1–8 / 8–15 / 16+. Place for gold and renown; strong finishes draw dojos, academies, and tutors.
  • Realms you can Visit for effects by place kind, and Travel to discovered lands.

Boundwake is free in alpha and in active development. Thanks for playing.
