# Stretch figure sources

All figures in this folder are cropped from:

**U.S. Army ATP 7-22.02**, *Holistic Health and Fitness Drills and Exercises* (October 2020, incorporating Change 1).

- Distribution restriction: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited.
- U.S. federal government work, in the public domain.
- Photos developed by the U.S. Army Center for Initial Military Training and produced at the Enterprise Media Center, Fort Eustis, Virginia.
- Official source: [Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/).

Each card in the app names the original figure (for example, Figure 16-1. RD1 Overhead Arm Pull). Crops remove page chrome and captions only. Movements were not altered.

The source PDF is not bundled with the app (`art/licenses/src/` is gitignored).

Flashcard line drawings live in `flashcards/` and are cropped from **U.S. Army FM 21-20**, *Physical Fitness Training* (30 September 1992, including Change 1, 1 October 1998). That manual is also a U.S. federal government work, marked approved for public release, and is in the public domain. See `flashcards/SOURCES.md`.
