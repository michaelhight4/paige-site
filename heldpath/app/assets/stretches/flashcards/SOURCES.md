# Flashcard figure sources

All figures in this folder are cropped from:

**U.S. Army FM 21-20**, *Physical Fitness Training* (30 September 1992, including Change 1, 1 October 1998).

- Distribution restriction: Approved for public release; distribution is unlimited.
- U.S. federal government work, in the public domain (17 U.S.C. § 105).
- Official source: [Army Publishing Directorate](https://armypubs.army.mil/).

Each card in the app names the original figure and manual page (for example, FM 21-20, page 4-6, Neck and Shoulder Stretch). Crops remove page chrome only. Movements and hold times were not altered.

The source PDF is not bundled with the app.
