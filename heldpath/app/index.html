Location while you walk or run

Heldpath uses your location to record your walk or run while a session is in progress, including when the screen is off or you switch to another app. A notification stays on your lock screen until you stop. Location is stored only on this device, is never sold or shared, and is not used for ads. Tracking stops when you stop the session.

You can read the full policy at paigegames.net/heldpath/privacy. The next screen is the system permission prompt.
