Your location

Heldpath uses your location to show where you are on the map, and to record a walk or run while a session is in progress, including when the screen is off or you switch to another app. A notification stays on your lock screen until you stop. Location is stored only on this device, is never sold or shared, and is not used for ads. Tracking stops when you stop the session.

You can read the full policy at paigegames.net/heldpath/privacy. The next screen is the system permission prompt.
