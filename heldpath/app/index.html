    Tracking

    Auto-pause when you stop

    When you stand still for a few seconds, Heldpath pauses so wait time is not counted. It starts again when you move. Turn this off if you want standing time included.

    Low-power tracking

    Uses a looser GPS fix so the phone lasts longer. The path may be a little less detailed. High-accuracy tracking stays on unless you turn this on.

    Count hills in distance

    Off by default. When this is on, distance includes the slope of hills using smoothed elevation. When it is off, distance is measured as flat ground.

    Hill-adjusted pace Free

    Show hill-adjusted pace on activities

    Off by default. When on, an activity can also show an effort-equivalent pace for its hills, using a published energy-cost model. Sector targets and goal times always use flat time.

    Offline maps

    When the map loads online, tile requests go to the map provider so streets can be drawn. They do not include your name or your walk and run history. Your location and activity stay on your phone.

      Integrations

      Each of these is off until you turn it on. Turning one off stops it immediately.

      Enhanced location (Google)

      Off by default. When on, Heldpath may use Google Play services for location, which can also use Wi-Fi and cell signals. Google may collect that location data. The default, and the fallback if you turn this off, is the phone’s GPS only. Nothing is sent to Paige Games.

      Health Connect read

      Off by default. When on, Heldpath may read steps, calories, heart rate, and workouts already stored in Health Connect on this phone, and show those numbers with your activity. This information stays on your device. Paige Games never receives it.

      Health Connect write

      Off by default. When on, Heldpath may write a finished walk, run, or ride (distance, time, and route) to Health Connect on this phone so it can appear in your health app. This information stays on your device. Paige Games never receives it.

      Health Connect background sync

      Off by default. When on, Heldpath may check Health Connect for new walks, runs and rides from the apps you already imported, and add them to the on-device store. Nothing is uploaded. You can turn this off at any time.

      Photon place search

      Off by default. Place search uses maps already on this phone. When this is on, Heldpath may send the place you type to Photon (photon.komoot.io) so results can be found outside a downloaded pack. Photon receives the search text, not your name or activity history. Paige Games never receives it. The app works fully with this off. The first time a search would use Photon, Heldpath will ask before sending anything. Turning this off stops those requests immediately.

      Overpass and live map tiles

      Off by default. Routing uses walkable paths already on this phone (a downloaded pack or the maps that come with the app). When this is on, Heldpath may send a map area to Overpass (overpass-api.de) or the map provider (OpenFreeMap) to look up tunnels or path data that is not already downloaded. Those services receive a map area, not your name or activity history. Paige Games never receives it. The app works fully with this off. The first time a feature would use it, Heldpath will ask before sending anything. Turning this off stops those requests immediately.

      YouTube videos

      Off by default. Stretch follow-along videos play from YouTube only when you turn this on and tap to watch them. YouTube may then receive that you asked to play the video. Heldpath does not load YouTube in the background, and it does not send your walks or location to YouTube. Paige Games never receives it. The first time you tap a video, Heldpath will ask before loading YouTube.

      Map tile cache

      Cached street tiles are stored only on this phone so the map can load faster or work offline. Clearing the cache removes those downloaded tiles. You can download an area again later.

      Route cleanup

      Snap finished routes to mapped paths

      After a walk or run, Heldpath can tidy the displayed route by snapping it to nearby mapped paths. This is off until you turn it on. It runs on this phone only, after you stop, and it does not change the raw GPX or FIT export.
