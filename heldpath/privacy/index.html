Heldpath

Privacy Policy

Heldpath · Paige Games · Effective date: November 20, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Heldpath app (“Heldpath”, “the app”), provided by Paige Games (“we”, “us”), handles information. Heldpath is a free walk and run tracker for Android.

In short: Heldpath does not require an account, does not include ads, and does not use analytics or tracking. Walks, runs, courses, and your location are stored on your device and are used only to record and show your activity, including while the screen is off during an active session. Optional Health Connect access stays on your device. Optional purchases go through Google Play. We do not sell or share your data.

1. No accounts, ads, analytics, or tracking

You can use every free feature of Heldpath without creating an account or signing in. We do not operate a user account system for this app.

The app does not include advertising, and we do not run analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of our own. We do not use your Advertising ID.

2. Information stored on your device

Heldpath stores the following on your device only:

  • Saved courses (names, pins, distances, and related details you create).
  • Recorded walks and runs (route, distance, time, pace, and related session details).
  • Calendar entries, progress charts, personal records, and settings (such as units and health-sync preferences).
  • Optional training plans you create or import, including goals and water-reminder preferences, and a local record of plan credits you have bought or redeemed.
  • A local record of an optional Support Us donation, used only so the app can confirm the purchase on this device.

This information is not sent to Paige Games. We cannot see it, and we cannot recover it if it is deleted from your device.

3. Location

Heldpath uses your precise location only on this device, and only to provide walk and run tracking:

  • To show where you are on the map.
  • To record the route, distance, and pace of a walk or run you start.
  • To show progress on a saved course, including distance done, distance left, and estimated time remaining.

If you start a walk or run and then turn the screen off or switch to another app, Heldpath continues to use your location only while that session is active, so the route can be recorded accurately. A notification stays on your lock screen until you pause or stop. Tracking stops when you stop the session. The app does not use your location in the background when no walk or run is in progress.

Location is stored on your device with the session it belongs to. It is not sent to us, not sent to a map server for tracking, and not used for advertising or analytics. We do not sell location data, and we do not share it with other users or third parties.

Map imagery can be kept on your device for offline use. If you download a map area, those tiles are stored on this phone and can be used offline. Downloading a map is optional and is separate from location recording. If you use the map online instead, Heldpath requests map tiles from the map provider (OpenFreeMap, and the bundled world basemap) so streets can be drawn. Those tile requests do not include your name, account, or walk and run history, and they are not used to collect personal data. Your location and activity stay on your phone.

4. Optional Health Connect access

Health sync is optional, free, and off until you turn it on. If you choose to connect, Heldpath may, with your permission:

  • Write completed walks and runs (such as distance, time, and route) to Health Connect so they appear in the health app on your phone.
  • Read steps and calories from Health Connect, and show those numbers next to your walks and runs. Heldpath does not calculate its own step or calorie counts.
  • Import walks and runs already recorded by a watch or another health app, so you can keep them in your history without using phone GPS.

Health and fitness information read from or written to Health Connect stays on your device and in Health Connect. We do not receive it, and we do not sell or share it. You can turn health sync off at any time in Settings, and you can withdraw Health Connect permissions in Android settings.

Heldpath is a fitness tracking and planning tool. Water suggestions that may appear in an optional paid plan are general guidance, not medical advice.

5. Optional in-app purchases

Heldpath is free to use. Optional purchases, if you choose to make them, are processed by Google Play:

  • A $5 Support Us donation, which is completely optional, unlocks nothing, and is only to help keep the app free. App access is and always will be free.
  • A $2 one-time plan credit for each new training plan you create. A plan you buy is yours to keep forever; you can save it, share it, and use it as you like. Importing a plan someone shares with you is free. This is not a subscription.

Google processes your payment under Google’s terms and privacy policy. We never receive your card or payment details. The app may receive a purchase confirmation from Google Play (such as the product, order status, and a purchase token) and stores it on your device so the donation or plan credit can be confirmed. Google may also provide us with transaction reports (such as order numbers, products, prices, and country) for accounting, refunds, and support. Purchase records in your Google account remain under Google’s control.

6. We do not sell or share your data

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share your location, health information, courses, or activity history with other companies, advertisers, or other users. The only third party involved in optional payments is Google Play, which processes the payment as described above. We may also disclose information if the law requires it, or to respond to a valid legal request.

7. Children

Heldpath is intended for teens and adults. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information (for example by email), contact us and we will delete that correspondence.

8. Deleting your data

Because your app data lives on your device, you control it:

  • Delete a saved course, session, or plan inside the app when that option is available.
  • Clear the app’s storage in Android settings, or uninstall the app, to delete remaining app data on that device.

We hold no account and no copy of your walks, runs, courses, location, or health data, so there is nothing of that kind for us to delete on our side. If you have emailed us, write to the address below and we will delete that correspondence. For purchase records held by Google Play, use your Google account controls or contact Google.

Owned plans should be exported if you want a backup. Consumable plan credits may not be restorable after you reinstall, which is a platform limit. An exported plan can be imported again for free.

9. Security

Your app data stays on your device and is protected by your device’s own security. Anyone with access to your device can see and change it. Optional purchases are transmitted by Google Play using Google’s security practices.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or US state privacy laws), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal data. Because we do not hold your app data on our servers, those requests are usually met by the deletion steps above. You can also contact us. For information held by Google Play or, if you enabled it, by Health Connect, use your Google or Android settings, or contact Google.

This policy is hosted on paigegames.net. The host may keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and the time of the request) for security and operations. That applies to visiting this page, not to using the Heldpath app. See the host’s privacy policy for details.

12. Changes

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update it here and change the effective date above.
