    The story

    In Lumara Spire, progress comes with a collar.

    The Brace clicks around a wrist and takes a piece of a person’s life force for a coin and the right to walk the streets. Most people take the trade. Lance, a barefoot orphan from the underbelly, has no one to offer him to the line. He learns early that the city is safer if it never learns his name.

    Then a baker finds him and gives him one. She also gives him the first warm room he has ever stood in. For a while the bakery is enough.
