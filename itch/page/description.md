# Catch Kilu. Beat 8 Masters. Face the Guardian.

**Kilumen: Start of a Journey** is a 2D creature-collecting RPG with a bright pixel look. Your new job at Revival Tech comes with a starter Kilu, a company phone and very high expectations.

## Your journey

Start in Brookhollow and head out along Trail 1. Walk into tall grass to meet wild Kilu, weaken them in turn-based or real-time battles, then hold up a Dominance Crystal to bring one onto your team. Beat each town's Master to earn their coin and open the next part of the world.

## Features

- **308 Kilu to discover.** Log every kind you find in your Kilindex and build a team of up to six.
- **8 Masters, 8 halls.** Each hall has its own puzzle, from plates and crystals in Fernwick to quench channels in Cindervale. Win all 8, then face the Guardian **Ondravel**.
- **Turn-based or real-time combat**, your choice. Team battles across elements like earth, water, light, wind, swamp, sea, spirit and fire.
- **A living world** with a day and night clock, weather, caves, water routes and fishing.
- **The Aurelith Games.** Town tournaments of three events in a row. Pick the right Kilu for each event.
- **Climb the company ladder.** Earn gold and merit and unlock perks on the RT Employee Benefits board.
