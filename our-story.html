Our Story

I began writing my book, Start of the End, with a story I couldn't let go of. But somewhere along the way I hit writer's block. Then I hit it again. And again.

Instead of forcing the words, I started reaching out to other creative ventures, anything that would push me past my own limits. That's how I found myself telling stories in places I never expected: in games, in music, and in webtoons. Every one of them taught me something new about building worlds and characters, and every one of them pulled me back to the page a little stronger.

This website is the result. It's my free access portal to my projects, built for the people who support them. Whether you found me through a game, a song or a comic, welcome. You're part of how all of this keeps going.

The Projects

Kilumen

Kilumen is a creature-collecting RPG with a chiptune soundtrack. You head out into the world, find and log creatures in your Kilindex, take on trainers and Masters, and slowly uncover the region and the company that runs so much of it. It's playful and full of characters, with a sense of humor that runs right through the game. Kilumen releases before the end of the year on Steam, Google Play and Apple.

Lifeline

Lifeline is a game I created because I loved life sims, but felt most of the market was thin on content. So I took everything I wished those games were and put it into my own. It's a text life sim you play one year at a time, right here on Paige Games, and every life you live turns into its own story.

Riftward

Riftward is currently being rebuilt. I took it down so I could give its art a full quality overhaul and rework its systems into something more complete, including a guild city-building side where guilds compete over resources. It'll be back when it's ready, and it'll be worth the wait.

Music

My music comes out under Paige Records. Writing songs is one of the ways I break through when the words won't come, and it's become its own world alongside the stories. Paige Records is on 20+ platforms, mostly EDM and pop, and I also write rock and country.

Webtoons

Webtoons are where my stories get told panel by panel. Here are all my series:

  • The Start of the End (13+, YA fantasy): the webtoon of my book. In Lumara Spire, progress comes with a collar. Read it on Webtoon Canvas: https://www.webtoons.com/en/canvas/the-start-of-the-end/list?title_no=1178955
  • After Annie (16+, zombie drama): the night the outbreak hit, Dave was at his coworker Annie's place. Now he has to get home to his wife and daughter. On Webtoon Canvas.
  • I Use My War Skill for Girls (16+, isekai rom-com): a half-wolf hero gets a war-planning skill and uses it to get dates instead of winning battles. On Webtoon Canvas.
  • Living in the Spotlight (teen sports rom-com): In production. Soccer prodigy Kai Mercer goes viral after one scouting trial, and his life changes fast.
  • My Divine Mission to Kill Demons (16+): In production. A Swedish meditator and martial artist is sent by the Archangel Michael to hunt down the false gods, pantheon by pantheon.
