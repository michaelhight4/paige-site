- Owner request 3 (Oct 18, 2026): AGE button jump. FIXED. Cause: the big round button was centred with `left:50%; transform:translateX(-50%)`, and the age-pop animation (`ls-age-pop`, art/theme.css) sets its own `transform`, which replaced the translateX while it ran, so every click flung the button ~46px left and rapid clicks kept restarting it. Now it is centred with `margin-left: calc(var(--age-d) / -2)` (no transform for layout), has fixed width/min/max/height (`--age-d`: 92px, 78px on short screens), `box-sizing`, no padding, overflow hidden, `touch-action: manipulation`. The animation only scales/rotates around its centre. Test: `tests/agebtn_ui.mjs` (real mouse clicks x8 at 3 viewport widths, samples the centre and layout size at 10/60/120/+150 ms; fails on the old CSS with a 46px jump).

### Team ownership + pack rules (Oct 18, 2026, America/Chicago)
- **Own a Team** (src/franchise.js, data/franchise.json via tools/build_franchise_data.py, ui/js/screens/franchise.js; Work menu, More directory, Your businesses): no pack needed (the Sports Owner pack only gifts a free team at 18+; teams are bought in-game). Choose sport (soccer, basketball, football, baseball, hockey), one of that sport's 12 unique fictional clubs (initial roll per club: strength, fans, finances, debt, stadium condition; stable per life), team type (semi-pro/pro/elite) and stadium starting size + tier. Each sport has its own 6-stat set: stat weights (0-10, total 30, preset or custom, re-tune once a year), training focus and style.
- **Staff**: head coach, scout/recruiter, medical and operations are required; assistant coach and marketing optional. Payroll (players + staff + stadium upkeep) is paid from team cash; owner backstop; 3 years of distress and creditors take the team. Yearly season (14 games), fans/attendance/revenue/reputation evolve with results; stadium size/tier/repair upgrades. Counts toward net worth. Tests: tests/franchise.mjs.
- **Pack gating**: Nepo Baby does almost nothing before 13 and applies in full on the 13th birthday; Free Real-Estate, Sports Owner (18), Rabbit Like (16), Golden Gun (crime age), Baller (14) and Einstein Mode (school level age) say clearly when locked. Tests in tests/premium.mjs.

### Batch 5b: create-a-club, logos, owned-team match flow
- **Create a brand-new club** (`Fr.create`, adapter `frAction('create', …)`, UI "✨ Create a brand-new club"): name (3–28 chars, can't reuse catalog names), sport, type, stadium size/tier/look (modern / classic / open bowl / garden park pitch, tiny gate modifiers), generated logo. Founding fee is 35% of an established club's price; the club starts thin, unknown (low rep/fans), debt-free.
- **Logos**: `logoSvg` builds slot-svg-v1 SVGs (5 shapes × 6 emblems × 8 palettes, `data-slot` primary/shade/emblem/accent, baked fills + CSS variable fallbacks, custom colours accepted). Bought clubs get an auto logo; `frAction('logo'|'look', …)` edits later.
- **Owned-team match flow**: `Fr.playHalf` / `Fr.playMatch` (halves with Poisson goals from team strength vs the strongest league rival, style atk/def, halftime tactic change) produce the same match record the coaching module uses, so the shared 2D `watch()` replay (now `watch(data)`) and halftime picker work for owned teams. The played game counts as one of the season's 14 (draws supported); unfinished halftimes clear at season end. Needs a head coach. Frames/status are RNG-pure.
