Privacy Policy

Applies to Lifeline (https://paigegames.net/play/) and Riftward (https://paigegames.net/riftward/), on the web and Android. Last updated: 2026-10-22.

Summary

You can play without an account. Game progress is saved on your device. Accounts and cloud saves are optional. The games show ads from Google, and in-app purchases go through Google Play.

Saves on your device

Progress and settings are kept in your browser's or device's local storage. We don't receive this data. Clearing your browser data or uninstalling the app deletes it.

Optional account and cloud saves

If you make an account, we use Supabase to store your email address (or the sign-in provider ID), your account ID, and your save data, so your progress can sync between devices. You can play without an account. To delete your account and cloud saves, email us (see Contact).

Ads

The games may show ads from Google AdSense (web) and Google AdMob (Android). Google and its partners may use cookies or device identifiers such as the Advertising ID to show ads, limit how often you see them, and measure them. Depending on where you live, you'll be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change that choice from the in-game privacy/consent option. You can also reset or opt out of the Advertising ID in your device settings. See how Google uses information from ads.

Purchases

In-app purchases on Android are handled by Google Play Billing. Google processes your payment details. We only receive confirmation of what was bought so we can unlock it.
