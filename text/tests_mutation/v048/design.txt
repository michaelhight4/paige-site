Kilumen : Start of a Journey




This game is modeled after a legendary series that no longer produces its classic style. The game
is a 2D 128 bit open world creature battler. That game is Pokemon, and its early releases up to its
2016 release is what we are studying for development. Game balance and mechanics, items,
building dynamic zones that house villages, towns and unique creatures and features.



A few key points:

Creatures: they should all have their own primary type and each has a counter and imunity. Also
some have sub types which can alter their counter and vulnerability. Main types include Earth,
fire, water, wind, Light, dark, mystic, legendary. Sub types could be grass, bug, sea or fresh
water, swamp, rain, magma, dragos, bird, spirit, ghost, demon, angelic, god and devil. We can
add more to make it make sense or balance the game. I expect 450 creatures with atleast 350

health, small for agility, cosmic for replacing shiny from pokemon, aggresive for attack boost.
These should all be reflected on their model so users know what variant they are by looking. For

the Kilumen world is. You work full time for a company who leads the charge on learning and

Ownership and economy: There is no inflation or any ability to own any buildings. The shops
prices stay fixed for the whole game and they are region based. You can sell extra beasts to your
company for money and progress towards your sell goals. Top sell goal is 2000 beasts.



Dark undertones: you use a dominance crystal to bond your soul to the kilumen, thats how you

skills after energy depletion causes imediate damage, higher chance to miss, and reduced
damage. Every battle action has a simple animation same as pokemon.
